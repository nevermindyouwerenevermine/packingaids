About Aidstra

👁️ Vision

  • A ready-to-go first aid kit business provides accessible, pre-packaged emergency medical supplies, focusing on compact design, clear labeling, and targeted utility.
  • Our vision is to provide portable, waterproof first aid kits equipped with flyers for immediate emergency response, ensuring everyone is ready for sudden disasters anywhere.

🎯 Mission

  • Our mission is to educate and empower people by providing accessible first-aid kits with easy-to-follow emergency guides. We aim to help individuals respond calmly and correctly during emergencies such as asthma, panic attacks, faintings, and other common health situation problems.
  • Our first aid kits (PackingAid) are designed with different variants to meet the needs of different age groups and audiences, such as children, teenagers, adults, the elderly; travelers, students, disabled people, etc.
  • We raise to promote safety and preparedness in communities—in all situations.

📌 Objectives

  • Develop first aid kits for different target markets such as students, travelers, athletes, adults, children, and families.
  • Offer affordable safety and first-aid products without compromising quality and reliability.
  • Increase awareness of the importance of carrying a first-aid kit everywhere through marketing and educational campaigns.

Core Values

Preparedness

Being ready before an emergency happens. At PackingAid, we believe that having a complete and organized first aid kit allows people to respond quickly and effectively during unexpected situations.

Humanity

We value compassion, empathy, and the well-being of others by providing products that help people respond to emergencies with care and confidence.

Safety

We are committed to providing reliable, easy-to-follow first aid kits(PackingAid) that help people respond quickly, calmly and correctly during emergencies.

Community

Community means helping and supporting one another by being prepared for emergencies and promoting safety for everyone.

Integrity

Being honest about the contents, quality, and purpose of each kit. Integrity is essential because customers depend on Packing aid Portable Kits to help them in emergencies and everyday situations
